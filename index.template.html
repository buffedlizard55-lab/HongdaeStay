How this was verified

The rule

An item is only allowed into an itinerary if its hours, address and (where relevant) its 2026 date were read directly from an official page: the operator's own site, VisitKorea, Visit Seoul, the venue's ticketing page, or the Seoul city body that runs it. Where a page prints no price, this site prints “not printed on the official listing” rather than a number from somewhere else.

What that ruled out

Two items that appear in most Hongdae guides did not survive: the Hongdae Free Market, whose organiser domain no longer serves it, and the old “Hongdae Playground” description, which the city replaced in December 2025. Both are documented in the Flags tab rather than quietly dropped.
